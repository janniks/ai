The decoder-only model you just built is the whole recipe: token embeddings plus positional information, a stack of transformer blocks with causally masked attention, a final normalization, and a projection to scores over the vocabulary. Training computes the cross-entropy loss, the penalty for assigning low probability to the true next token, at every position of every sequence in parallel; the causal mask, which hides each token's future, is what lets one pass through a sequence of $T$ tokens supply $T$ prediction problems at once.

Generation inverts this. Feed a prefix, take the predicted probabilities at the last position, sample a token, append, repeat. The model is a pure function from context to next-token probabilities; the loop around it is where text comes from.
