An embedding is a lookup: token number $i$ maps to row $i$ of a big table of numbers, and that row, a list of a few thousand learned values, is the token's vector. The rows are adjusted during training like every other part of the model, and tokens that occur in similar contexts are pushed toward similar vectors, because that is what makes prediction easy.

The result is that meaning becomes geometry. Similarity between two vectors is measured by the angle between them, near zero for close meanings, and some relations appear as consistent offsets: stepping from the vector for king by the difference between woman and man lands near queen. Word2vec learned static vectors, one per word; the transformer's contribution, next chapter, is to make each token's vector depend on its context.
