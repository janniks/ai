The induction head is the proof of concept: a two-head circuit that finds the previous occurrence of the current token and copies what followed it, completing the pattern A B then later A, so predict B. These heads appear abruptly during training, coincide with a visible bump in the loss curve, and account for a substantial part of in-context learning, a mechanistic explanation of a capability that otherwise looks emergent.
