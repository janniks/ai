- Generates a `REVIEW_GUIDE.md` of clustered files, reviewed one checkbox at a time.
- Calibrates on the first file; touched files are semi-locked at their confirmed wording.
- Your corrections (and manual edits, diffed) become explicit rules.
- Rules fan out across the remaining files via sub-agents; finished rules land in `CONVENTIONS.md`.
