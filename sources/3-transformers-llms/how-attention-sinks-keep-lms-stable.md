# How Attention Sinks Keep Language Models Stable
Language models fail on long texts because they remove special "attention sink" tokens that hold unused attention. Keeping these sink tokens fixes the problem and lets models handle much longer sequences. This discovery improved model stability and influenced new designs in AI systems and quantization methods.
