# Diffusion Models
Diffusion models are used for image generation and denoising by blending noise into clean data points iteratively. These models learn to map data to a normal distribution over multiple steps, improving image synthesis compared to traditional methods like GANs. Diffusion models have applications in various fields, including medical image reconstruction and video processing, offering advanced performance and generalization capabilities.
