# Andrew Ng — Machine Learning Specialization (Coursera)

The canonical beginner ML on-ramp. Updated version of Ng's 2012 course
(4.8M+ learners). Balances intuition, hands-on coding, and just-enough math.
Beginner level, ~2 months at 10 hrs/week.

## Courses in the specialization

1. **Supervised Machine Learning: Regression and Classification** (~33 hrs)
2. **Advanced Learning Algorithms** (~34 hrs) — neural nets, decision trees, practical advice
3. **Unsupervised Learning, Recommenders, Reinforcement Learning** (~28 hrs)

## Covers

Supervised & unsupervised learning, linear & logistic regression, neural
networks, decision trees, clustering, recommender systems, intro RL,
transfer learning, responsible AI. Tools: NumPy, scikit-learn, TensorFlow,
Jupyter.

> The accessible entry point. Step up to CS229 (`stanford-cs229-machine-learning.md`)
> for the mathematical depth.
