# Harvard Stat 110: Probability

The rigorous probability course (the index's biggest gap). Joe Blitzstein's
full Harvard lectures, free on YouTube, with a free online textbook
(*Introduction to Probability*, Blitzstein & Hwang) and an edX version
(Stat110x). Probability as the language underneath all of ML.

## Resources

- Lectures (YouTube, ~34 videos):
  https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL2SOU6wwxB0uwwH80KTQ6ht66KWxbzTIo
- Free textbook: http://probabilitybook.net
- edX (free signup): https://www.edx.org/course/introduction-to-probability-0
- Official site + strategic practice problems: https://stat110.hsites.harvard.edu/

## Topics

Probability axioms, combinatorics, conditional probability & Bayes, random
variables, expectation/variance, named distributions (Binomial, Poisson,
Normal, Exponential, Beta, Gamma), joint distributions, covariance &
correlation, transformations, convolutions, MGFs, law of large numbers &
CLT, Markov chains.

> Watch *after* Seeing Theory for intuition; this is the depth pass.
