# Eigenvectors and Eigenvalues explained visually
Eigenvalues and eigenvectors help us understand various systems, like electrical circuits and population dynamics. When we multiply a vector by a matrix, eigenvalues indicate how the vector changes in size or direction along a specific line, called an eigenspace. These concepts can also extend to complex numbers, leading to spiraling patterns in certain systems.
