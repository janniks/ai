# Inside the Matrix: Visualizing Matrix Multiplication, Attention and Beyond
The visualization tool mm is based on the concept that matrix multiplication is fundamentally a three-dimensional operation, helping to establish correct relationships between argument shapes and result shape. Simple examples and animations are used to build intuition before delving into more complex matmul expressions, such as matrix-vector products and vector-matrix products. The tool extends visualization to compositions of matmuls, including right-associative and binary expressions, providing a clear way to understand and parallelize compound expressions. Additionally, the document explores visualizing GPT2 attention heads to observe the structure, computation, and values of these components, offering insights into the structured information they produce and the potential for more efficient computational means.
