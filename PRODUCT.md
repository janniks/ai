# Product

## Register

product

(Hybrid: a progress-tracking *tool* presented with editorial-content styling. The check-off interaction and its states follow product rules; the visual atmosphere is editorial.)

## Users

Self-directed learners who want to understand how LLMs actually work. They arrive curious but without a syllabus, and want a single trustworthy path through the best existing videos, articles, books, papers, and interactive demos. Context: studying at their own pace over days or weeks, returning repeatedly, wanting to see what they've covered and what's left.

## Product Purpose

A standalone, check-off-able curriculum at `/course`: a long, chaptered reading/watching list of real resources on how LLMs work. Learners mark items as seen/read; completed items collapse so the remaining path stays in focus. Required ("core") material forms a spine; "background" and "advanced" items are optional detours, visible but recessed. Progress is local only (no accounts, no backend, faking a checkmark is fine). Success: a learner can open it, immediately grasp the path, and track real progress across sessions.

## Brand Personality

Scholarly, calm, confident. Three words: considered, literate, uncluttered. It should feel like a beautifully typeset self-study book curated by someone who knows the field, not a SaaS dashboard or a gamified course platform. No badges, streaks, or dopamine mechanics.

## Anti-references

- Cream/sand/parchment "editorial-warm" AI default backgrounds.
- Gamified LMS chrome (Duolingo streaks, XP bars, confetti, mascots).
- Generic SaaS course cards: identical icon + heading + text grids.
- Cluttered MOOC dashboards (Coursera/Udemy density and upsell).

## Design Principles

- **The path is legible at a glance.** Structure (chapters, core spine, optional detours) reads instantly without instruction.
- **Completion clears the way.** Checking something off should visibly reduce remaining load, not just toggle a box.
- **Optional means recessed, not hidden.** Background/advanced material stays present but quieter, never competing with the required path.
- **Typeset, not decorated.** Hierarchy and calm come from type, spacing, and restraint, not ornament.
- **Honest tool.** No accounts, no tracking, no fake stakes. Progress is the user's, stored locally.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 (recessed optional items must still meet it). Full keyboard operation of check-off and chapter collapse, visible focus, correct checkbox/disclosure semantics and state announcements. `prefers-reduced-motion` honored for every transition (collapse, check, reveal). External links labeled and opened safely.
